---

Small Group Activity (SGA) projects are often underrated. Ours started with a simple question: **why does moving one metric ton from the mill to the port cost what it does?**

## 1. Map the current state

We broke the export inland journey into stages — loading, trucking, depot handling and port delivery — and attached a cost and a lead time to each one.

## 2. Find the real drivers

Using a Pareto analysis, we found that a handful of factors drove most of the cost:

- Empty-return trips from the port
- Waiting time at loading bays
- Unbalanced allocation between trucking vendors

## 3. Improve and standardise

We introduced round-trip planning, staggered loading schedules and a vendor allocation rule based on performance data.

> The result: a sustainable reduction of **$2.5 per MT** in export inland cost.

The lesson? Improvement doesn't need a huge budget — it needs good data and a team willing to question "the way we've always done it."
