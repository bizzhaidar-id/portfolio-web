---

Finishing a management trainee program as one of the **top 4 performers in the first year** taught me more than any classroom. Here are five lessons I still use.

### 1. Learn the process before improving it
Spend time on the floor. The best ideas come from operators who live the process every day.

### 2. Numbers build credibility
A clear before-and-after number convinces people faster than any presentation.

### 3. Ask "why" five times
Most problems you see are symptoms. Keep digging until you reach the root cause.

### 4. Communicate early and often
Bad news shared early is a problem to solve together. Bad news shared late is a crisis.

### 5. Own your development
Certifications, side projects and mentors don't come to you — you have to go after them.
