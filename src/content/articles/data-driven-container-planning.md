When you oversee more than **2,000 box containers every month**, intuition stops scaling. Here are the habits that keep our operation predictable.

## Start every day with one dashboard

A single Power BI view shows planned vs. actual dispatches, truck availability and port cut-offs. If it isn't on the dashboard, it isn't managed.

## Manage by exception

Instead of tracking every truck, we flag only the ones that break a rule:

1. Late to the loading bay by more than 30 minutes
2. Stuck at a checkpoint longer than the standard time
3. At risk of missing a vessel cut-off

## Close the loop weekly

Every week we review the top three delays, assign an owner, and check that last week's actions actually worked.

Data doesn't replace people on the ground — it tells them where to look first.
